Raid Downtime

Pulls your logs from GW2 Wingman and shows how much of a raid was spent fighting and how much idle.

Times are in your local time zone. Leave Start empty to begin at the first pull and End empty to stop at the last fight.

Extra source: dps.report

The token stays in your browser and goes only to dps.report. Only fights that include the account above are counted.
